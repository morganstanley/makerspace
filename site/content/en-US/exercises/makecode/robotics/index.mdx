## Intro to CPX Robotics with MakeCode

This robotics pathway covers sensor-driven coding with the Circuit Playground Express and MakeCode. The curriculum is designed for students who want to explore movement, inputs, and simple autonomous behavior with block-based coding.

### Included courses

- [Cutebot with MakeCode](/exercises/makecode/robotics/cutebot)

The Cutebot material has been brought into this repository so it can be maintained alongside the rest of the Makerspace curriculum.
